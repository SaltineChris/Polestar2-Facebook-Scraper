# PRODUCT.md — Polestar 2 NZ Listings Tracker

## Product Truth
- **Core User & Purpose**: A personal tracking tool for a single buyer in New Zealand actively hunting for a used or dealer Polestar 2 electric vehicle across Facebook Marketplace and TradeMe Motors.
- **Primary Job to Be Done**: Immediately see which Polestar 2 cars are for sale in NZ, spot price drops, identify exact drivetrain trim (SRSM, LRSM, LRDM, Performance), compare prices, and open the active listing with zero friction.
- **Aesthetic Direction**: Minimalist Scandinavian / Polestar brand inspired. Monochrome/deep charcoal foundation (`#101418` dark / `#F8F9FA` light), crisp geometric typography (Inter / system-ui, tabular numerals for prices), clean borders, understated gold/Swedish accents (`#E5A93C` / `#D48800`), zero AI-template fluff (no serif mixed with loud multi-colored borders, no unnecessary decorative emojis or novelty cards).
- **Core Surface**: Single-page dense vehicle directory. Compact feed with clear thumbnails, bold price/trim badges, location, date, and direct outbound links. Secondary market analytics available when needed, without crowding the primary feed.
